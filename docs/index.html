Session Slides Organizer

Put every speaker’s slides in one list, in speaking order, and open each talk when it’s time.

Files stay on this computer: nothing is uploaded. The list is kept in this browser until you clear it.

    1. Add each talk. Drop in decks from a USB stick, email or Dropbox, or paste a link to Google Slides or another online deck.
    2. Put them in order. Drag rows into speaking order and give each a clean title.
    3. Present. Open in browser shows the deck full screen, one slide at a time, with clicker support. Press ? there for shortcuts.
    4. Keep the session. Download a ZIP of the decks, numbered in order, and drop it back in later to pick up where you left off.

    * Decks open in a built-in viewer and may not match PowerPoint exactly. Download one to present from PowerPoint itself.

    † Saved from Google Slides as a PDF: each slide appears in its finished state, without animations or transitions, and video and audio don’t play.

    Links open on their own site and need the internet. For Google Slides, Get PDF keeps a copy here for offline use. A link straight to a PDF or PowerPoint file is downloaded when the site allows it.
