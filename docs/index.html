    Clear the list?

    To keep a copy, download the session ZIP first. You can drop it back in later.
    1. Add each talk. Drop in slides from a USB stick, email, Dropbox, etc., or paste a link to an online presentation, such as Google Slides.
    2. Put them in order. Drag rows into speaking order and give each a clean title.
    3. Present. Open in browser shows a presentation full screen, with a pop-up slide picker and clicker support. Presenter view adds a separate audience output if you have multiple screens. ? shows keyboard shortcuts.
    4. Keep the session. Download a ZIP of all the presentation files, numbered in order. Use it on any computer, or drop it back into slideorg.org to pick up where you left off. The list should also stay in this browser until you clear it.

    * PowerPoint slides open in a built-in viewer with fewer features, and may not match PowerPoint exactly. To present in PowerPoint on this computer, click Download and open the file.

    † Saved from Google Slides as a PDF: each slide appears in its finished state, without animations or transitions, and video and audio don’t play.

    Links open on their own site and need the internet. For Google Slides, Get PDF keeps a copy here for offline use. A link straight to a PDF or PowerPoint file is downloaded when the site allows it.
